The physical network is the part of the system nobody wants to think about until it fails. Structured cabling covers the runs between workstations, wireless access points, cameras and the network rack, terminated so the space can be maintained and extended later.

Pricing for standard CAT6 and CAT5e pulls is published on the pricing page. Larger or more complex installations are quoted after a site survey.
